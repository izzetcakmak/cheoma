The Insight · 기와불사 giwa-bulsa

For centuries, before a Korean temple hall rises, givers donate its roof tiles — and ink their wishes onto them. The wish is sealed into the building, sheltering everyone beneath it.

In Korea, a wish becomes part of the roof.

Web3 has plenty of speculation. It lacks belonging — a thing you add to, not trade away. CHEOMA brings this living ritual on-chain.

The Product · 처마

One shared roof.
2,048 wishes.

  • Lay a tile on a communal 64×32 roof — pick any open spot.
  • Choose a glaze — your color, fired forever.
  • Ink a wish — up to 140 bytes, public, permanent.
  • Each tile is a fully on-chain ERC-721: sumaksae SVG art & metadata generated inside the contract. No IPFS. No server.

The roof is the social object. One tile is fragile; together they make a roof.

01 · CULTURAL DNA

The chain's name, made physical

GIWA is literally named after the roof tile. A communal tile roof isn't an app on GIWA — it is the chain's own metaphor turned into a product.

02 · SPEED = FEELING

A tile lands instantly

1-second blocks + Flashblocks (~200ms preconfirmations). Your tile appears before your finger leaves the screen. A communal canvas is unusable on 12-second chains.

03 · WALLET-READY

Consumer-simple by design

One tap, one color, one wish — no DeFi vocabulary. Exactly the mechanic GIWA Wallet placement is made for.

04 · DOJANG ROADMAP
